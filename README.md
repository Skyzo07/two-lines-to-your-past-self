[Two Lines to Your Past Self on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca)

## Entry

`main.ts` writes exactly two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> Tell your mother the truth in the hospital corridor before the doctor comes back.
> You said "I'm fine" instead, and she died believing the lie you told to spare her.

Deterministic, no network, no clock, no randomness. It fails loudly if the text is not 1–280 characters or not exactly two lines.
